Pages

  • Tracker · log meals by text, voice or photo; log blood-sugar readings; browse the daily log by day; 7-day history.
  • Journal · entries imported from Family Alignment for your email.
  • Report · printable 7, 14 or 30-day nutrition and blood-sugar report (Print / Save PDF).
  • Search · find meals, readings and journal entries; add format=json for machine use.
  • Export JSON · download the last 30 days.

Sign-in

Use Sign in with Gudmundr: one sign-in for every Gudmundr app. Your meal history is kept by email, so it carries over from the old Google sign-in. Access to Meals is granted by the Gudmundr admin.

AI estimates

Estimates are produced through the Gudmundr Gateway (IBM Granite first, with fallbacks) and are approximate, based on USDA FoodData Central. Blood-sugar values are self-reported. This is not medical advice.

Layout

Pages are on the left (bottom on phones); ecosystem apps and appearance (auto, light, dark) are on the right. Use the pin buttons to keep either rail pinned; the choice is remembered. High contrast is at the top of each page.
